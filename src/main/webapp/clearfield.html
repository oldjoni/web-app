What We Do

End-to-End Compliance
Advisory Services

Whether you're a solo agent managing renewals or a startup MGA building from the ground up, Clearfield Advisory delivers the regulatory expertise you need to operate with confidence.

Compliant Sales & Marketing Materials

We review, draft, and certify your sales scripts, brochures, digital ads, and illustrations against state and carrier guidelines — so your marketing never puts your license at risk.

Regulatory Audits

Proactive internal compliance audits that identify gaps before regulators do. We deliver a full findings report with prioritized remediation steps, giving you a clear path to a clean record.

MGA Setup & Structuring

Launching a Managing General Agency? We guide you through the entire compliance framework — entity formation, regulatory registration, contracting hierarchy, and operational compliance policies.

State Licensing Support

Resident and non-resident licensing, CE compliance tracking, license renewals, and Department of Insurance correspondence — handled accurately and on time, every time.

Carrier Contracting Guidance

We decipher carrier appointment requirements, contracting hierarchies, and compliance obligations — ensuring your agreements align with state law and your business model.

Ongoing Compliance Monitoring

Stay ahead of regulatory changes with continuous monitoring of state bulletins, NAIC model law updates, and carrier policy shifts — with real-time alerts and quarterly compliance reports.
