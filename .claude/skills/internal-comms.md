# Internal Communications Skill

Draft various types of internal company communications with structured guidelines and examples.

## Supported Communication Types

- **3P updates** (Progress, Plans, Problems)
- **Company newsletters**
- **FAQ responses**
- **Status reports**
- **Leadership updates**
- **Project updates**
- **Incident reports**

## How It Works

Three steps: identify your communication type, load the matching example file from the `examples/` directory, and follow that file's specific formatting and tone instructions.

## Key Guideline Files

- `examples/3p-updates.md` — team progress-plans-problems formats
- `examples/company-newsletter.md` — organization-wide announcements
- `examples/faq-answers.md` — question-and-answer responses
- `examples/general-comms.md` — catch-all for other communication needs

For communication types not explicitly covered, provide additional context about your preferred format.
