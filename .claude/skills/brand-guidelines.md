# Anthropic Brand Guidelines Summary

This resource provides Anthropic's official brand identity specifications for styling artifacts.

## Key Brand Elements

**Color Palette:**
The guidelines specify a dark color (`#141413`) for primary text, a light shade (`#faf9f5`) for backgrounds, and three accent colors: orange (`#d97757`), blue (`#6a9bcc`), and green (`#788c5d`).

**Typography Standards:**
"Applies Poppins font to headings (24pt and larger)" while body text uses Lora, with Arial and Georgia as fallbacks respectively.

## Primary Features

The skill automatically applies brand colors and typography to various artifacts. It intelligently selects fonts based on text hierarchy, cycles through accent colors for shapes, and adapts to available system fonts without requiring additional installation.

## Use Cases

This styling guide applies to projects requiring "brand colors or style guidelines, visual formatting, or company design standards."
